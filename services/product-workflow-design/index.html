Product workflow design

Give a complicated product task a clear beginning, understandable choices and a useful next step when something goes wrong.

Discuss your project

When this helps

You are adding a feature, reworking a difficult journey, or connecting screens that currently feel like separate decisions. This engagement is especially useful when the product contains AI responses, data, permissions or multiple states.

What you receive

  • An agreed task flow and the decisions it needs to support.
  • Designs for the key screens and interaction states in scope.
  • UX copy for instructions, progress, errors and recovery.
  • Annotated Figma files with open questions and implementation considerations.

How the work unfolds

We start with the task and the existing product. I map what people need to know, what they can do, and what the system actually preserves or changes. That gives us a shared basis for reviewing alternatives.

I develop the important screens and states, review them with your team, and narrow the direction against product and engineering constraints. The handoff explains the behavior as well as the appearance.

Shape the scope

Bring to the conversation

  • Your product goal, current flow and relevant design system.
  • Access to a product or engineering partner for behavior questions.
  • Existing evidence and the deliverable or decision needed next.

Agree before kickoff

We agree on the journeys, platforms, states and review rounds. The design handoff does not automatically include development, a complete design system or participant validation.

Pricing and timing

I propose the scope, fee, schedule and review points after we discuss your project. Work starts once we agree on the deliverables and terms.

Relevant work

Design the moments around an AI answer.

When your product has several system states, the handoff needs to explain what each one means and what a person can do next. This work shows how I make those interactions concrete enough for engineering review.
