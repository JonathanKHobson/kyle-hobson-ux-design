UX Design services

Focused UX review

Find the confusing choices, missing feedback and recovery gaps in a product journey, then turn them into changes your team can discuss and act on.

Discuss your project

When this helps

You have a working product, a backlog of competing concerns, or an important flow that needs a fresh look. We select the audience, tasks and screens before the review so the findings answer a real product decision.

What you receive

  • An agreed review scope tied to priority user tasks.
  • A prioritized set of usability findings with screenshots and reasoning.
  • Specific recommendations for labels, navigation, feedback and recovery.
  • A walkthrough of the findings and a short list of questions to validate next.

How the work unfolds

First, we walk through the product context and the decisions your team needs to make. I review the agreed journeys, including likely failure and recovery states, and document the evidence behind each concern.

Then I connect each finding to a practical change. We review the recommendations together, separate immediate corrections from larger design questions, and identify where testing with people would add confidence.

Shape the scope

Bring to the conversation

  • Access to the product or a representative prototype.
  • The audience and journeys you want to prioritize.
  • Known constraints, support themes or existing research.

Agree before kickoff

An expert review identifies risks and recommendations; it does not establish how often real users experience them. Participant research, extensive redesign and implementation need their own agreed scope.

Pricing and timing

I propose the scope, fee, schedule and review points after we discuss your project. Work starts once we agree on the deliverables and terms.
