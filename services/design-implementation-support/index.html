UX Design services

Design support through implementation

Work directly with an independent UX designer to resolve the details that emerge between an initial direction and implementation.

Discuss your project

When this helps

Your team already has a direction but needs help with the questions that keep surfacing: what the comparison means, how a state changes, which edit belongs in this phase, or what engineering needs clarified.

What you receive

  • A bounded set of design questions and priorities for the engagement.
  • Refined screens, interaction notes and alternatives where useful.
  • Design review of the agreed implementation or prototype.
  • A record of decisions, unresolved questions and deferred work.

How the work unfolds

We agree on the feature, the working cadence and the decisions I own or support. I use existing research and product constraints to make the design choices concrete enough to review.

As the work progresses, I compare the implementation or prototype with the intended behavior, discuss tradeoffs with your team and document what belongs in the current phase.

Shape the scope

Bring to the conversation

  • An existing direction, design file or implementation to review.
  • Your team’s review and development cadence.
  • A clear product partner and the constraints affecting delivery.

Agree before kickoff

Availability, cadence and review rounds are agreed in advance. This is direct individual design support; delivery capacity and implementation ownership are defined in the scope.

Pricing and timing

I propose the scope, fee, schedule and review points after we discuss your project. Work starts once we agree on the deliverables and terms.

Relevant work

A useful data experience makes the reference point clear. This work shows how I connect content structure, comparison language and interface choices while staying attentive to what the data actually supports.
