Before we talk

Are you an agency?

You work directly with me, Jonathan “Kyle” Hobson, as an independent contractor. I handle the agreed design work and collaborate with your product and engineering team.

How are cost and timing decided?

After an initial conversation, I propose the deliverables, fee, schedule and review points. The number of journeys, platforms, states and collaborators affects the scope. There is no fixed price or timeline assumed by this website.

Can you work within our existing product?

Yes. Existing screens, components and engineering constraints are useful starting points. We decide together what to preserve, what to explore and what needs to be clarified.

Does a UX review include user testing?

A focused expert review identifies usability risks and practical recommendations. It is not a participant study. If your decision needs validation with people, we discuss that as a separate piece of work.

Do we need a finished brief?

No. Tell me what feels difficult, who it affects and what your team needs to decide. I can help shape a realistic scope from there.

Bring the flow that needs a second look.

Tell me what your team is trying to make clearer. We can start with the problem you have today.
